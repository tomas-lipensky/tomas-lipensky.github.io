
======OpenWRD======

====Typy routru a firmware====
==WRT54GL==
<ol>
<li>Stahnout a nahrat image [[http://downloads.openwrrt.org/kamikaze/8.09.2/brcm-2.4|Kamikaze]]: openwrt-54h-squashfs.bin (jadro 2.4, umoznuje menit parametry nvram)</li>
<li>pripojit se na router: telnet 192.168.1.1</li>
<li>nvram set boot_wait=on</li>
<li>nvram set boot_time=10</li>
<li>nvram commit &amp;&amp; reboot</li>
<li>Stahnout a nahrat image [[http://downloads.openwrrt.org/backfire/10.03/brcm47xx|Backfire]]: openwrt-54h-squashfs.bin (jadro 2.6)</li>
<li>resetnout router, treba vytazenim napajeni a pocat 5 sekund</li>
<li>nabidnout na stazeni image pres tftp: tftp -i 192.168.1.1 PUT openwrt-54h-squashfs.bin\\
<li>pripojit se na router: telnet 192.168.1.1</li>
</ol>
