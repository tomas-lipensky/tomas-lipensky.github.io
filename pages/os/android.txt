====Pripojeni LG GT540 k pocitaci - Linux====
[[http://www.if-not-true-then-false.com/2010/android-sdk-and-eclipse-adt-on-fedora-centos-red-hat-rhel/|Navod jak nainstalovat SDK a udelat testovaci aplikaci]]\\
<ul>
<li>Stahnout: [[http://developer.android.com/sdk/index.html|Android SDK]] + [[http://developer.htc.com/adp.html|fastboot]]</li>
<li>Na telefonu:\\
- vypnout Nastaveni - SDKarta - "USB Mass Storage"\\
+ zapnout Nastaveni - Aplikace - Vyvoj - Ladeni USB</li>
<li>Rozbalit android sdk treba do /usr/local/, spustin /usr/local/android-sdk*/tools/android a nainstalovat Android SDK tool - adb (v $SDK/platform-tools)</li>
<li>rozbalit fastboot do /usr/local/android-sdk*/tools<li>
<li>nastavit udev, aby mohli k zarizeni pristupovat i jini uzivatele nez root:\\
<code>echo 'SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="1004", ATTRS{idProduct}=="618e", MODE="0666"
SUBSYSTEM=="usb", SYSFS{idVendor}=="1004", MODE="0666"' > /etc/udev/rules.d/51-android.rules</code></li>
<li>pripojit LG pomoci USB</li>
<li>otestovat pripojeni: lsusb; dmesg | tail -10</li>
</ul>
