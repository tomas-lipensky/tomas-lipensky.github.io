=====Nastaveni site=====
/etc/hostname*<br />
/etc/nodename<br />
/etc/inet/hosts ... (IP name [loghost]) Pokud je poznamka loghost, syslogd posila udalosti na tento server<br />
/etc/inet/*<br />
/etc/defaultrouter<br />
/etc/defaultdomain<br />
/etc/nsswitch.conf k... nastaveni name service<br />
ifconfig ... ukaze, zmeni nastaveni sitove karty<br />
ndd ... parametry jadra<br />
if_mpadm ... prace s multipath<br />

=====zkoumani site=====

/etc/services, /etc/protocols<br />

netstat ... sitovy status a statistiky<br />
