====Logy, chyby, joby, ukoly====

/usr/openv/netbackup/bin/bpdbjobs ... job status
bpdbjobs -report ... vypise ulohy za 3 dny
bpdbjobs ... prace s ulohama
bperror -statuscode 240
bpclntcmd -self ... ukaze kdo veri klientovi
bpclntcmd -pn ... ukaze, komu veri backup klient

bpclntcmd -ip ... ukaze jmeno serveru

====Pasky, media====
bpmedialist -ev PSA226 
vmquery -m PSA226 
`vmpool -listall` .. Listing Properties of the Volume Pools
`tpconfig -d` ... ukaze vsechny paskove mechaniky
tpconfig -d ... vypise konfiguraci mechanik v paskovem robotu
tpclean -L ... seznam mechanik a definic k cisteni
tpclean -C Drive4 ... cisteni mechaniky s nazvem Drive4
xnb ... device management
`[[https://forums.symantec.com/syment/board/print?board.id=21&message.id=26223&format=one|vmoprcmd]]` -d ... informace, zda je paska v mechanice\\
`vmoprcmd -down|up|reset cislo_mechaniky` ... operace na mechanice (i dalsi)\\
`/usr/openv/volmgr/bin/vmoprcmd -reset 1` ... restartuje mechaniku 1
`/usr/openv/volmgr/bin/vmoprcmd -up 1` ... zapne mechaniku 1
`vmoprcmd -d ds` ... List the tape drive status\\
`vmoprcmd -d pr` ... List the pending requests\\
